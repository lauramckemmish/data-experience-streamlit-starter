# Contributor credit review

Use the shared contributor-credit decision in `playbook/decisions/contributor-credit.md`.

For this resource only:

1. Identify the people who may deserve named intellectual credit.
2. For each person, establish what perspective or intellectual contribution actually shaped this resource or the approach behind it.
3. Distinguish what they did from what materially influenced the resource.
4. Reduce each contribution to the shortest accurate contribution type, preferably 2–4 words.
5. Reuse the shared contribution vocabulary where it fits.
6. Do not inherit contributors or intellectual lineage from another resource without establishing that the contribution genuinely applies here.
7. Flag uncertain cases rather than inventing a rationale.
8. Order the final named list alphabetically by surname.
